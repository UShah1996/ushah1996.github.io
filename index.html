AFib screening from wearable ECG

CardioWatch

Undiagnosed AFib is a preventable cause of stroke. I trained a CNN-LSTM on hospital ECGs and a simpler heartbeat-timing model, then tested both on devices they had never seen.

What I found: the deep model wins in the lab, then swings widely on other devices. The simple model varies about 3 times less. Lab accuracy did not predict real-world behavior.
