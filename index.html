Health AI · live demo

CardioWatch: AFib screening from wearable ECG

What it does
Screens for atrial fibrillation (AFib), a hidden cause of stroke, from wearable heart readings like the Apple Watch's.
What I did
Trained a deep model and a simple heartbeat-timing model, then tested both on 4 device datasets neither had seen.
Result
The deep model wins in the lab but drops on new devices. The simple one varies about 3 times less.

Search · zero-shot

HyDE: fast search without labeled data

What it does
Finds the right passage among 100K documents with no labeled training data, by drafting a likely answer first and searching for text that matches it.
What I did
Built the search pipeline and tested 6 index sizes to find the best balance of search quality and memory.
Result
An index 96% smaller that still finds 71% of the right passages, answering in 14 ms.

I came to San José State for my MS to go deeper. Today I like the systems side of ML most: making models fast, reliable, and cheap to serve.
